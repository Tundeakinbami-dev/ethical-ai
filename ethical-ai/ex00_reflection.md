On the other hand, if i use AI to geberate the code, at least not a complex one and study it before submitting it, then i can explain the code without the help of AI.

If AI was unavailable during an exam or job, then i would answer the questions i could answer without the help of an AI, thus, the need for me to learn programming languages well.
